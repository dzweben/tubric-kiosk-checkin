Overview

The Challenge: Multi‑lab workflows often create fragmented intake records, inconsistent identifiers, and ad‑hoc data sharing.

The Solution: A unified intake and harmonization layer that standardizes records, assigns stable GUIDs, and supports IRB‑safe coordination through de‑identified exports and controlled system‑to‑system sync.
